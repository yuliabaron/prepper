Prepper Telegram Assistant

A personal, single-user assistant

Prepper lets its owner manage their own Google Calendar, Google Docs and Google Sheets by sending plain-language messages to a private Telegram bot. For example: “create a meeting on Thursday at 3pm and invite these people”, or “add a line to my calculations sheet”.

Who can use it

Only its owner. The bot ignores messages from anyone else. Prepper is not offered to the public and does not accept sign-ups.

How it works

  • Every change (creating, editing or deleting an event, or editing a document or sheet) is shown as a preview first and runs only after the owner taps “Do it”.
  • It accesses only the owner's own Google account, with the owner's permission.
